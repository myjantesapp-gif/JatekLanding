---
name: JATEK legal publishing
description: Non-obvious legal-content constraint for the JATEK public site and Hostinger package.
---

JATEK's public legal pages should remain explicit about what is known and what still needs official confirmation. Do not invent the publisher's legal name, registered address, registration number, or Hostinger contract details.

**Why:** Legal notices and RGPD pages are trust-critical; plausible-looking invented company details can make the site misleading or non-compliant.

**How to apply:** Before commercial publication, replace the clearly marked incomplete hosting/editor details with the official records, then rebuild the Hostinger static package so the archive and live pages stay synchronized.
